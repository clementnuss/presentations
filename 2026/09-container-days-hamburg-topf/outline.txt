## 1. Why Talos Linux
- Talos intro: 12 binaries, immutable, minimal, secure

## 2. The ClusterAPI Dead-End
- Our migration plan: kubeadm+Ansible → CAPI+Talos
- Siderolabs deprioritized CAPI providers (low priority, focus on Omni)
- Technical concerns: K8s as dependency, in-place upgrades, CAPI complexity
- Alternative approaches: Omni, Ansible, purpose-built tool

## 3. Introducing TOPF
- The "thing" — early whiteboard sketch
- What TOPF is: single Go binary, full lifecycle management
- Layered YAML patch model: all/ → control-plane/ → worker/ → node/<host>/
- `topf.yaml` configuration
- Dry-run & pre-flight checks
- Upgrades with TOPF
- GitLab CI integration (dry-run on PR, apply on merge)
- TOPF vs alternatives (talosctl scripts, Ansible, Omni)
